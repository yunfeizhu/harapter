# Code of Conduct

Harapter is committed to a welcoming, safe, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, security disclosures, and project boundaries.
- Give credit and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sustained disruption, sexualized
conduct, and publishing private information without permission are not allowed.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
security-advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict
participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and made
without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
